# Pakish.ORG launch SEO, GEO, content, and conversion plan

Date: 2026-08-08

## Outcome

Position Pakish.ORG as a trustworthy, women-focused technology training institute that attracts qualified applicants through classic search and AI-assisted discovery. The site should make three facts immediately clear: the programs teach practical IT, AI, and freelancing skills; learners can attend in Karachi, Lodhran, or online; and regular admission is fee-based with limited need-based support under the Fi Sabilillah quota.

## Evidence baseline

- The live site returns HTTP 200 and exposes robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
- Google has indexed an older title that still says "Free IT & AI Training", so the next release must reinforce the corrected fee/subsidy position and request recrawling after deployment.
- Google Pakistan autocomplete shows active demand around "AI course in Karachi" and variants including fees, near me, free, and online. Women-focused long-tail results are comparatively thin, creating a differentiation opportunity.
- Exact monthly search volume is not claimed because no authenticated Keyword Planner, Search Console, or paid keyword dataset was available during this pass.
- The live sitemap still contains `/campus/korangi`, although the physical location and rendered copy are Gulshan-e-Iqbal. This weakens local entity consistency.
- Most pages reuse one generic social image. The homepage has only Organization and WebSite schema, while its visible FAQ and programs are not represented in structured data.
- The homepage repeats the same three programs in multiple large sections, includes unverified testimonial identities, and presents fixed income ranges without first-party outcome evidence.
- The admission flow collects personal information and can share a subsidy application with trusted donors after consent, but the site has no dedicated privacy page.
- The launch baseline used an unsupported Next.js 14 patch with critical published advisories. A Next.js 15 test still retained bundled high-severity advisories, so the implementation moves to the current supported Next.js 16 line and its ESLint flat configuration.

| Insights index | women in tech Pakistan | generative AI, freelancing, remote careers, Pakistani women in technology |

The homepage primary phrase deliberately balances relevance and attainable competition. The higher-volume local phrase "AI courses in Karachi" belongs on the Karachi campus page to avoid homepage/campus keyword cannibalization.

## Homepage search presentation

- Meta title: `IT & AI Courses for Women in Pakistan | Pakish.ORG`
- Meta description: `Join practical IT, AI and freelancing courses for women in Pakistan—at Gulshan-e-Iqbal Karachi, Lodhran or live online. Fee-based; need-based support.`
- H1: `IT & AI Courses for Women in Pakistan`
- Hero support: explain mentor-led, project-based learning, three attendance modes, paid admission, and limited eligibility-based support without overpromising income.

## Phase-one implementation

1. Align title, description, H1, internal anchors, and visible copy with the keyword map.
2. Consolidate duplicate program sections so visitors choose among three clear paths once, with admission as the primary conversion action.
3. Replace unsupported earnings and unverifiable testimonial claims with portfolio, mentor, and learning-process proof.
4. Move the Karachi campus to `/campus/gulshan-e-iqbal`, retain a permanent redirect from `/campus/korangi`, and update canonical, sitemap, navigation, and internal links.
5. Add route-specific branded 1200x630 OG images generated by a restrained Next.js image endpoint for the homepage, admission, privacy, campuses, insights index, and every article.
6. Add extractable structured data for the educational organization, programs, homepage FAQ, campuses, breadcrumbs, and articles without inventing ratings, prices, coordinates, or credentials.
7. Add `llms.txt` as an emerging GEO discovery aid, while treating it as non-guaranteed and secondary to crawlable HTML and structured data.
8. Add a plain-English privacy page and contextual admission privacy notice that accurately describes WhatsApp/email submission and consent-based donor review.
9. Remove placeholder social links and strengthen real entity/contact signals.
10. Apply the supported Next.js upgrade, then run lint, build, production audit, metadata/schema checks, responsive browser validation, reduced-motion checks, and admission-flow validation without submitting real personal data.

## Measurement after deployment

- Verify the deployed commit and all canonical/redirect/OG URLs.
- Submit the sitemap and request recrawling of the homepage and new Gulshan-e-Iqbal route in Google Search Console.
- Record baseline impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position for the mapped query groups.
- Configure privacy-reviewed analytics only with an approved measurement ID and consent approach; do not add silent tracking by assumption.
- Sample Google AI results and ChatGPT/Perplexity-style answers monthly for brand mentions and citations, treating GEO visibility as an emerging measurement rather than a guaranteed ranking outcome.
- Reassess title/description after 28 days of impression data; do not redesign again without evidence.

## Definition of done for local review

- One indexable canonical per page, one H1, unique title/description, and a working unique OG image.
- Old `/campus/korangi` permanently redirects to the canonical Gulshan-e-Iqbal URL.
- No false universal-free-course claim, fixed income promise, fake rating, placeholder social link, or unconsented donor-sharing claim remains visible.
- Lint and production build pass; dependency audit no longer reports a critical Next.js vulnerability.
- Desktop and mobile browser checks show a coherent hierarchy, restrained animation, keyboard-friendly navigation, and a usable admission flow.
