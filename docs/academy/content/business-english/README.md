## Module 1 — Professional introductions & clarity

### Lesson 1.1 — Introduce yourself with purpose

**Learning objective:** Deliver a 45–60 second introduction: who you are, what you do, what you need.
**Practice:** Record or partner-practice; peer checklist for clarity, pace, and filler words.
**Exercise:** Two versions — networking vs workplace standup.
**Self-check:** Can a stranger repeat your “ask” after hearing you once?
**Instructor key:** The ask must be explicit (meeting, feedback, introduction).
**VIDEO NOT RECORDED**

### Lesson 1.2 — Clarify without sounding unsure

**Learning objective:** Use clarification phrases that keep authority (“To confirm…”, “Just so I understand…”).
**Exercise:** Rewrite five vague statements into clear ones.

## Module 2 — Email and workplace messaging

### Lesson 2.1 — Request and follow-up emails

**Learning objective:** Write a clear request email and a polite follow-up.
**Practice:** Pair or self-record speaking of the same message; peer checklist for clarity and tone.
**Exercise:** Transform a blunt message into a professional version without losing the ask.
**Formative:** Identify three tone problems in a sample thread.
**VIDEO NOT RECORDED**

### Lesson 2.2 — Chat / WhatsApp professionalism

**Learning objective:** Adapt email clarity to short messages without sounding rude.
**Exercise:** Three message threads (ask, nudge, decline) under 40 words each.
