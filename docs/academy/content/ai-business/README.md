## Prerequisites / outcomes

**Prerequisites:** Basic spreadsheet and email skills; manager permission to discuss workplace processes.
**Outcomes:** Map workplace processes; draft AI-assisted SOPs; propose a low-risk automation pilot with verification steps.

## Module 1 — Workplace AI opportunity mapping

### Lesson 1.1 — Where AI helps (and where it must not)

**Learning objective:** Identify three tasks suitable for AI assist and three that must stay human.

**Prerequisites:** Course onboarding complete.

**Concept:** Good candidates are repetitive text/data tasks with clear success criteria. Poor candidates involve irreversible actions, regulated decisions, or unverified client facts.

**Realistic example:** Drafting meeting notes from a transcript you already have (assist) vs approving a discount in WhatsApp without a human (must stay human).

**Guided practice:** Fill a process inventory table: task, frequency, risk, AI suitability, verification owner.

**Self-check:** Define “human-in-the-loop” in one sentence for your workplace.
**Instructor key:** A required human review or approval before an action that affects customers, money, or records.

**Exercise deliverable:** Completed inventory for at least eight tasks.
**Rubric:** Coverage 30%; risk judgment 40%; clarity 30%.
**Common errors:** Marking payroll or refunds as “fully automate”; vague task names.

### Lesson 1.2 — Stakeholder brief

**Learning objective:** Write a one-page opportunity brief a manager can accept or reject.

**Concept:** Problem → current cost → proposed assist → risks → ask.
**Exercise:** One-page brief for your highest-scoring inventory item.
**Rubric:** Decision-readiness 50%; honesty about risk 30%; brevity 20%.

**Formative quiz (5):** (1) Name a high-risk task. (2) Name a low-risk assist. (3) Who owns verification? (4) What data must not leave approved tools? (5) What is the rollback if the pilot fails?

## Module 2 — Document & meeting workflows

### Lesson 2.1 — Meeting notes that stay truthful

**Learning objective:** Turn a transcript or notes into an action list without inventing commitments.

**Concept:** AI drafts; humans confirm owners and deadlines. Unknowns stay marked TBD.
**Exercise:** Produce notes + action table from a provided sample transcript (instructor supplies).
**Rubric:** No invented commitments 50%; action clarity 30%; format 20%.
**Common errors:** Fake attendees; invented due dates.

**VIDEO NOT RECORDED**

### Lesson 2.2 — SOP drafting with AI

**Learning objective:** Draft a standard operating procedure with steps, owners, and exception paths.

**Exercise:** SOP for one recurring team process (≤2 pages).
**Rubric:** Steps runnable by a new hire 40%; exceptions 30%; AI-assist notes 30%.

## Module 3 — Spreadsheet and reporting assists

### Lesson 3.1 — Clean data before you ask the model

**Learning objective:** Prepare a small dataset and ask for summaries without pasting regulated columns into unapproved tools.

**Concept:** Column hygiene, sample rows, and redaction beat dumping entire sheets into a chat.
**Exercise:** Redact a sample sheet; produce three summary questions and answers with verification notes.
**Rubric:** Redaction 40%; question quality 30%; verification 30%.

**VIDEO NOT RECORDED**

### Lesson 3.2 — Narrative from numbers

**Learning objective:** Write a short management narrative from a table without inventing trends.
**Exercise:** Half-page narrative + “what we still need to confirm” list.
**Common errors:** Causal claims without evidence.

## Module 4 — Pilot design, risk, and change management

### Lesson 4.1 — 30-day pilot design

**Learning objective:** Propose a 30-day pilot with success metrics, data handling rules, and rollback.

**Exercise:** Pilot one-pager: scope, users, metrics, data rules, rollback, training plan.
**Rubric:** Scope realism 25%; metrics 25%; data rules 25%; rollback 25%.
