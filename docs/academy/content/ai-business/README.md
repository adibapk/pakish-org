# AI for Business & Workplace Automation — foundation draft

Slug: `ai-business` · Status: unpublished foundation · VIDEO NOT RECORDED  
Readiness: Foundation draft — not launch-ready

## Prerequisites / outcomes

Prereq: basic spreadsheet and email skills. Outcomes: map workplace processes; draft AI-assisted SOPs; propose a low-risk automation pilot with verification steps.

## Modules

1. Workplace AI opportunity mapping  
2. Document & meeting workflows  
3. Spreadsheet and reporting assists  
4. Pilot design, risk, and change management  

Each module includes: intro paragraph, one sample lesson (objectives + teach + exercise), formative quiz (5 items), and a capstone outline (department automation brief with risk register).

### Sample lesson (Module 1)

**Objectives:** Identify three tasks suitable for AI assist and three that must stay human.  
**Teach:** Good candidates are repetitive text/data tasks with clear success criteria; poor candidates involve irreversible actions, regulated decisions, or unverified client facts.  
**Exercise:** Process inventory table (task, frequency, risk, AI suitability).

**Formative check:** Define “human-in-the-loop” in one sentence for your workplace.

**Capstone outline:** Propose a 30-day pilot with success metrics, data handling rules, and rollback.
