## Primary-law research and implications for review

1. The [Sindh Consumer Protection Act, 2014, as amended](https://sja.gos.pk/assets/Acts_Ordinances_Rules/Sindh%20Consumer%20Protection%20Act,%202014.pdf) addresses faulty services (sections 13–17), misleading representations about services, price, qualifications and affiliation (section 21), and advance disclosure of a seller's return/refund policy (section 20). Section 17 does not allow statutory service liability to be excluded by contract or notice. **Inference:** give every learner the applicable cancellation/refund terms before taking payment, even though counsel should confirm how section 20's seller wording applies specifically to training services. Never use a blanket statutory-rights waiver or undisclosed "no refunds" rule.
2. The [Sindh Private Educational Institutions (Regulation and Control) Ordinance, 2001](https://www.sindhlaws.gov.pk/Setup/Publications_SindhCode/PUB-16-000141.pdf) defines an institution broadly enough to mention technical, professional and vocational education (section 2) and provides for registration (sections 3–6). **Open legal question:** whether and how it applies to Pakish Institute's Karachi physical/online formats, and which authority/certificate is relevant. Verify the existing registration evidence before any claim of regulatory approval or first public cohort.
   The [SBTE official site](https://www.sbte.edu.pk/) lists short-course functions; the owner's pending application must not be described as completed affiliation or recognition. Pakistan-qualified counsel should confirm whether SBTE affiliation, a separate Sindh educational-institution registration, or both are required for the proposed format and certificates.
3. The [Electronic Transactions Ordinance, 2002](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administratordbc98dd49f2df3b1d07bb986dcceb9a3.pdf) recognizes electronic records and sets conditions for accessible, accurate retention (sections 3–6). **Operational inference:** retain each learner's dated written offer, policy version, acceptance, payment confirmation and refund correspondence; do not rely on a mutable web page alone.
4. The [Ministry of IT legislation register](https://www.moitt.gov.pk/Legislations) lists the 2020/2023 Personal Data Protection Bills as drafts. Do not describe a draft as enacted law. Independently improve the current privacy notice and recording-consent workflow on their own merits.

## Recommended commercial policy for owner/counsel decision

The owner says instructors can be paid on the same day and has chosen **no discretionary change-of-mind refund after payment, including before a learner's first session**. Choose a **short public Terms page plus a separate Payment, Cancellation & Statutory Remedies page**, with the exact version linked in each written fee confirmation *before payment*. This owner preference cannot override remedies required by law for non-delivery, faulty or materially misrepresented services. A sentence promising "no refunds under any circumstances" is therefore not approved for publication. Individual cohorts and bespoke B2B work need different explanations. This avoids an indefinite "ask staff" policy and gives a consistent, auditable answer in WhatsApp, email, admissions and the payment page.

### Owner-selected individual live-cohort rule, subject to mandatory law

- **After payment:** no voluntary refund for a learner's change of mind, availability conflict, missed classes or request to switch courses, whether the first session has started or not. State this conspicuously in the dated written offer *before* requesting payment. The operational reason (same-day instructor payouts) is internal and need not be a public justification or a purported waiver of consumer rights.
- **Transfers/rescheduling:** staff may offer a different cohort or another suitable course, but this is discretionary unless the written offer or applicable law provides otherwise. Record any learner acceptance in writing; do not describe a transfer as cash restitution when a refund is legally owed.
- **Pakish cancels or materially fails to deliver:** do not force a credit or invoke the change-of-mind rule. Offer a reasonable replacement only if the learner accepts it. Assess any refund, price reduction, damages or other remedy required by applicable law, including the value of undelivered services.
- **Material change** (course format, instructor, schedule or syllabus compared with the written offer): notify the learner promptly and offer an appropriate remedy. Do not silently substitute a recorded course for promised live classes.
- **Complaint/remedy operations:** acknowledge a request within two business days; decide within seven business days after obtaining necessary details; issue any approved or legally required refund within fourteen business days using the original channel where practicable. Provider/bank posting time may vary. Provide a written reason for the decision.
- **Need-based support:** any legally required refund relates only to the learner's actual paid contribution; sponsor contributions follow the separate sponsor arrangement. Do not pay a sponsor-funded amount to the learner.

### Proposed custom team/onsite rule

Require a signed or clearly accepted statement of work identifying preparation deliverables, instructor commitments, dates, total fee, payment milestones, cancellation/rescheduling rule and any genuine non-recoverable third-party costs. The owner prefers no voluntary refund after payment for customer-side cancellation; make that explicit before payment and have counsel review its enforceability in each B2B context. Pakish-caused non-delivery remains subject to the agreed remedy and applicable law. Do **not** silently apply an individual-course policy to bespoke work.

## Proposed public Terms of Training — counsel-ready structure

The public Terms should be plain English, versioned and concise. The written course offer should control the commercial particulars but must not remove statutory rights.

1. **Provider and scope.** "Pakish Institute" is the training brand of **[verified Pakish Group legal name and form]**, at **[verified business address]**. These Terms apply to paid training, website and Academy access; custom-team SOWs may add course-specific terms. They do not turn an inquiry into an enrollment. FBR registration or a pending SBTE application must not be presented as educational accreditation.
2. **Admissions and order of documents.** Applying is free and does not reserve a seat. Before payment Pakish gives a dated offer stating the course title, learning outcomes, instructor (if confirmed), format, live-session count, start/end dates, timezone, final fee/taxes, payment schedule, included materials and the policy version. Enrollment follows acceptance and verified payment; Academy access is then provisioned by invitation.
3. **Delivery.** Live online classes use a confirmed meeting platform (currently Google Meet); the offer states any in-center/onsite component. A reasonable reschedule may be offered, with prompt notice and a remedy for material non-delivery. A recording is included only if the written offer expressly says so.
4. **Fees and cancellation.** Starting prices on marketing pages are not the final quoted fee. Pay only to the verified recipient on the written offer. Receipt upload does not verify payment. The linked Payment, Cancellation & Statutory Remedies Policy governs. There is no voluntary change-of-mind refund after payment; this does not remove remedies required by applicable law for Pakish's non-delivery, faulty or materially misrepresented services.
5. **Learner responsibilities.** Accurate enrollment details, respectful conduct, lawful use of tools, no sharing of Academy credentials or distributing teaching materials. Provide an accessibility/support contact and a fair warning/appeal process for serious misconduct.
6. **Materials and intellectual property.** Pakish/its licensors retain ownership; the enrolled learner receives a personal, non-transferable learning-use licence for the access period stated in the offer. Do not imply ownership of third-party tools or materials. A learner keeps rights in their own work while granting Pakish only the permission necessary to review/grade it; no marketing use of work or likeness without separate consent.
7. **Recordings, AI and privacy.** Do not record identifiable live participants until advance notice and separate informed consent/alternative participation arrangements exist. Students must not submit other people's confidential data to third-party AI tools. Link the current Privacy Notice and specify support/contact channels.
8. **Outcomes.** No job, earnings, IELTS score, accreditation or certificate is guaranteed. State certificates or exam preparation only where actually included in the course-specific offer. This does not reduce the duty to deliver the advertised training with reasonable care.
9. **Concerns and disputes.** Written complaint to **[verified support address]**; acknowledge within two business days, investigate and respond with a proposed resolution within seven business days. Preserve access to applicable consumer authorities/courts; do not impose exclusive arbitration or a jurisdiction clause that removes mandatory rights.
10. **Changes and records.** Publish new versions prospectively. The version accepted with a learner's offer remains available to that learner. Keep dated evidence of offer, acceptance, receipts, enrollment and any refund decision under an approved retention policy.
